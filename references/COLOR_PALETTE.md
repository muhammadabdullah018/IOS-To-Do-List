# Skyday color & background system
- Primary Sky Blue: `#0593C6` — primary progress card, active tabs, action feedback.
- White: `#FCFFFF` — clean content cards and reading areas.
- Hot Pink: `#D83995` — streak counter, limited celebration, key add button.
- Supporting: `#12364A` ink, `#EAF8FD` pale surfaces, `#DDF5FE` sky, `#628092` secondary text, `#DDECF4` borders.

## Background alternatives
1. **Today: Pixel Sky Dawn (chosen)** — pale cyan sky wash at header; three tiny original pixel clouds floating behind empty space; opaque card in foreground; atmospheric fade into white scroll area. Keep illustrations outside the text safe zone.
2. **Lists: Clean Cloud Paper** — white main canvas, subtle cyan header, one stepped cloud shape top-right; no background motif behind rows.
3. **Progress: Pink Sunset Accent** — cyan-to-white upper backdrop; subtle pink decorative corner star behind streak module, not behind text.

Illustrations must be original; no stock sky photos or unlicensed sprites. Compare against `design/` mockups for composition, but use native, accessible sizing rather than screenshot tracing.
