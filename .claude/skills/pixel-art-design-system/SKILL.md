# pixel-art-design-system

Core colors are exactly #0593C6 (sky blue), #FCFFFF (white) and #D83995 (hot pink). Derive lighter tints and shadows only when necessary; never silently replace brand hues.

Create reusable SwiftUI sky background components: Today has layered atmospheric cyan gradients/tints, intentionally stepped pixel clouds and restrained stars, fading into a clean white task surface. Lists is quieter; Progress has celebratory pink details. Respect safe areas, scroll behavior and contrast.

Treat art and UI as distinct systems: pixel-art clouds, small mascots and badges may be pixelated; readable text and interactive controls should remain crisp, native and high contrast. Specify pixel grid sizes and nearest-neighbor scaling for raster art. Favor reusable vectors/shapes when possible.

Check screenshots at real device resolutions. Never tile noisy pixels behind dense text. Avoid random stock imagery, excessive decorative overlays, inconsistent pixel scales or full-page neon gradients.

Deliverables: documented design tokens, color/spacing/corner radius usage, sky component variants, asset naming rules, screenshot-based quality checklist.
